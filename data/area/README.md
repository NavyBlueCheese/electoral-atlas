# Constituency areas, 2026

`constituency_area_km2_2569.csv`: area of each of the 400 constituencies in km², computed from the 2026
boundary polygons (Thai-ECT-election-map, MIT) on the Lambert cylindrical equal-area projection
(`scripts/constituency_area.py`). Total 517,176 km², within 1% of Thailand's land area. The boundaries are
reconstructions, not a legal reference, so small areas (central Bangkok) carry the most relative error.
Used for population density (eligible voters per km²).
